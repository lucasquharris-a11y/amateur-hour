Logo directions

Five simple ideas for Amateur Hour, each shown on paper and on green. All are rough placeholders built from basic shapes, to pick a direction before anyone draws it properly.

1. The punched o

The o in Hour is the dot punched out of the card. It ties the name to the punch card, works as a plain wordmark, and the dot alone is the app icon. This is the placeholder now in the nav.

2. The one-handed clock

A clock with only an hour hand, pointing at seven in the evening. Literal about the name, and it says "one evening a week" without words.

3. The participation ribbon

The rosette you got for showing up. It owns the joke in the name: nobody here is winning, and that is fine.

4. The wonky gold star

The sticker a teacher gives a beginner, drawn slightly off. Warm and a bit childlike, and it would make a good stamp on the card.

5. The rubber stamp

A round ink stamp with the city in it, like a library or rec-centre mark. The most local and the most old-fashioned.

My pick is 1 for the logo, with 4 or 5 kept as a secondary stamp for the punch card and merch. 1 is the plainest, needs no separate symbol, and already belongs to the card.
