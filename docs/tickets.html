Three ticket directions

Each one is built to say "one or the other" before you touch it. Click an option to see the pick; click it again to reset.

A. One strip, tear one off

The two classes are a single strip of raffle tickets, joined by a perforation. A strip only comes apart one way, so it's obvious you keep one half.

B. One ticket, punch your pick

A single "admit one" ticket that's good for either class, with a hole to punch next to each. One ticket can only admit you once, and the punch is the same one that ends up on your card.
