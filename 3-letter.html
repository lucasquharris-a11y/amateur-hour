glassblowing, Thurs

Work asks less of your head every month, and most evenings go to a screen. A normal week doesn't hand you many chances to learn something from scratch anymore.

So every Sunday we text you two small classes around San Francisco, for things you've probably never done. You pick one and show up.

You won't like all of them. You will have used your hands and met eight people.

Join in San Francisco

This week it's one of these two.

Blow a glass cup

Thursday at 6:30pm, at Public Glass in the Bayview. Six people around one furnace.

or

Catch crab off the rocks

Saturday at 9am, on the San Francisco coast with Sea Forager. Rock crab, a poke pole and a casting net.

That's the whole decision. There's no catalogue to scroll and there are no reviews. Bring a friend on a day pass if you'd like company.
