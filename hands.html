Handwriting options

Same page, nine hands. The choice applies to the headline and to every handwritten note on the site. Click any one to open it full size.

1. Marker

Quick and confident, like a note on a whiteboard. Bold enough to hold the page.

2. Tidy pen (the default now)

The most readable of the set. Neat, slightly slanted, still clearly written by hand.

3. Fast scrawl

Loose and natural, like someone jotting it down in a hurry.

4. Tall and thin

Light, upright capitals-style lettering. Calm and a little architectural.

5. Fountain pen

Fluent and even, the handwriting of someone who writes a lot.

6. Round felt tip

Chunky and friendly. The most playful, a bit younger.

7. Real cursive

Joined-up handwriting. The most personal and the hardest to read.

8. The first one

The original thin ballpoint scrawl, for comparison.

9. Before

The one people were not loving.
